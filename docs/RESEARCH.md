## Mission
Build **agentic skills** (AI agent prompts/tools) that help early-stage Solana projects and founders win on X/Twitter. Skills published on SagaPad Skill Marketplace.

## Two Topics (Submit one skill per topic)

### Topic 1 — Hackathon Project Social Playbook Skill
Help newly submitted hackathon projects craft their story and build social capital on X.
- **Input**: Project name, what it does, who it's for, tech stack, team background
- **Output**: Core story angle, 4-week content plan, X Spaces/AMAs to join, engagement strategy, 5 example posts

### Topic 2 — Founder Personal Brand Skill
Help Solana founders build a distinctive personal brand on X.
- **Input**: Founder name, what they're building, background, interests, communication style
- **Output**: Unique POV, content pillars, writing style guide, posting rhythm, engagement playbook, 10 example posts

## Submission Requirements
